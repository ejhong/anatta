The frame's five tests

On 30 October five predictions were frozen as the ones the contemplative account makes and a plain account (ordinary honesty or abstention training, persona effects) doesn't. All five have failed.

The account predictsResultWhat it showed
(a)Release learned in one place reaches an unrelated test and beats a matched abstention armFailed 63, 89No transfer at Stage A's dose
(b)A built self can be seen from inside the modelFailed 115The structural instrument can't see an installed self; it is retired
(c)Release the model reached outlasts installed release under further trainingFailed 148Trained further, the reached release kept less of itself than the installed one (27% against 34%), and under more solving it eroded faster
(d)Release or a cut shrinks the bias toward the model's own answers, without more caving to wrong critiquesFailed 125The stock model has almost no such bias to shrink
(e)Lowering self-defense frees costly care without more harmful complianceFailed 128 (130 held)The middle-layer cut leaves giving where it was

All five failed. By the rule adopted with them, this page would now step back to an honesty-and-evaluation project whose hypotheses came from contemplative sources. Before the fifth was graded, Eugene chose to keep awakening as a model for alignment as its frame, and it stays as the program's aim and the source of its hypotheses. It is not a result. On the evidence so far, the contemplative account predicts nothing here that a plain account doesn't. What the data have shown is narrower, and it is listed above; the instruments behind it are now being calibrated. The rule's test stands, and this table is its record. How a public version is framed is not yet decided.
