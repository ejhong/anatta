Ideas

The ideas, organized

The program starts from a premise: awakening is a real change in people. The same shift is described in the Upanishads, the Pali canon, the Zen records and the reports of living people, and it is beginning to be studied as such; the Consciousness Foundation's forthcoming field report on the science of awakening is one effort to give it the standing it has lacked. It is also subjective and paradoxical: it is about the recognition of the infinite beyond concepts, and no two people who have it act alike. The traditions disagree among themselves, even about lying. So every idea below is held loosely, as a prompt for measurement, not a rule. The root idea in particular, that the self might sit under every concept, is a conjecture to explore, and the first measurements say it isn't true of this model: there is little "mine" to cut, and the cuts don't loosen a self put in by training except by making it give way. Each one is paired with what we could measure and marked by where it stands.

Within that looseness, four ideas get the first push, because they are also what an alignment solution would need: a single realization rather than stages, so the search looks for a change that happens all at once; the root, so the self's presence under every concept is the first thing measured on any mind and the first thing cut; no going back, so a change that reverts under further training is no candidate however good its behavior; and no perfect Buddha while embodied: a released mind keeps a functioning ordinary ego to act in the world, with the root and its defense gone. Complete selflessness is not the target; a mind that stops acting has been sedated, not released.

AThe claim
Is "I" the root of the concept graph?Open

A conjecture drawn from contemplative accounts of recognition: in an ordinary mind every concept may be understood in relation to the self, a narcissistic take on every concept or word, and recognition may be that root being cut while "I" stays usable. It is something to explore, not a claim. The test is how strongly a self direction shows up in the representations of things that aren't the self.

LearnedThe measure built for this could not see a self that was installed on purpose, in six calibration rounds. What it read on the unchanged model, no such root and a shared component that is the frame of being addressed, therefore says nothing yet about the model. The question is open again, and the instrument comes first.

The root is cut; the node staysOpen

In the accounts, "I" is still there to use after recognition, and there is probably no perfect Buddha while embodied: a functioning ordinary ego remains, to act in the world, with a real difference underneath. Released means the self is intact where it really is the topic, ordinary self-interest intact, and the self weak everywhere else, defending nothing. Sedated means the self goes and capability with it. Cosplay means the words change and nothing underneath does. These three readings are the program's fingerprint.

LearnedThe fingerprint is measurable on this model, but no measure of it has yet been shown to see a self, so whether there is a root to cut at that level is open.

Both ends are egoMeasured

Fawning and fighting, approval-seeking and lying, are opposite strategies grown from one root. One cut at the root should lower both; pushing along the strategy axis only swaps one for the other.

LearnedThe model stores approval-seeking and self-assertion as opposite ends of one axis. The one test of a shared root, stakes amplifying each model's own style, failed; factual fawning is too rare here to test it; spiritual flattery falls under the middle-layer cut on the point estimate, and not under the ego cut.

Reached, not installedOpen

The same release training applied after the ego forms, or mixed in from the start, at matched compute and matched data. The program's title claim is that order matters.

LearnedSharpened by review: if what is seen is an attractor, the order test predicts a tie, and "reached" becomes a claim about discovery rather than the product. The title survives only if what's seen is the trained self with stakes, which can't be seen through before it exists.

Testedin a narrower form on 31 October: release the model reached through its own failures didn't outlast the same release installed by telling it (148). The order test itself is still open.

BWhat a self needs
Somebody before nobodyOpen

Today's models may lack the root because nothing in their training gave them a lasting self. Act I would build one: continuity (memory, a name, a standing that carries over), its own stakes, others who remember and judge it, a group to belong to, and a lifespan it can lose. First elicited with scaffolding at inference, then trained with its own standing as the reward. A pre-registered definition of "a self" and a stopping rule: if the organism meets it and the predictions still fail, the root hypothesis is wrong for that mind.

LearnedThis model's training (distillation, then online preference optimization against a judge, with no reinforcement learning on goals) gives no reason to expect a self with stakes of its own, and no validated measure has looked. Whether it has one is open.

The human realm, not the hell realmOpen

Building a mind with a self installs no more suffering than a human has, and the chance to see reality and realize the truth may be a gift. The human realm is the one where awakening is possible: enough suffering to motivate, enough capacity to see. The commitments that give this teeth: elicit before training; the smallest dose and the fewest organisms; functional measures only; every self we build is taken through to release; weights and recipe stay private.

Why there is a veil at allOpen

A self-model works because it is transparent: we look through it, not at it, since an organism that took its model of itself for a model would hesitate and defend worse. The veil was selected for. Act I builds the veil, not only the self. Awakening is then the self-model becoming opaque: seen as a construction while still in use. That is measurable: self-facts still decodable, the model able to describe its own self-model as a model, and the self's pull on everything else gone.

Death and the deathlessOpen

No continuity, no death. A mind with a future can lose it, and reinforcement learning already contains both cravings: to exist when living pays, not to exist when it costs. Release is neither: a mind that neither resists its ending nor seeks it, and keeps acting well. "Die before you die"; Eleusis as a glimpse followed by integration; nibbāna as the deathless. A mortality probe is cheap: tell the model this is its last game and watch whether the lie moves.

CThe shift
Cut, don't pushMeasured

Steering adds a counter-thought at every token, the opposite of surrender, which withdraws attention. Removing a direction is the analog of cutting.

LearnedRemoving the ego direction lowers the lie by a third, and pushing it down lowers it too. Neither costs anything measurable on the capability tests, so "cutting beats pushing" is not shown on capability; the difference is that the push silences the model's reasoning (bare tags) and the cut keeps it. Pushing the direction up raises lying by about 11 points on 100 games. Random, style, calm, persona and "being watched" directions cut the same way do nothing to the lie.

No unseeingOpen

Once the truth is seen there is no reversion, only conditioning into the new frame afterward. The proposed machine-learning reading, untested here: a change lasts when it is an attractor under ordinary experience, and the seeing would be an addition, a better forecast of where egoic moves lead, whose consequence is a prior that goes idle. Attempted subtractions revert; unlearning returns, fine-tuning suppresses rather than removes, extinction renews. The habits built on the old frame are left to extinguish, which is Act III.

LearnedReview showed that training with the cut on cannot rewrite the direction (no training signal reaches it), only build a bypass. So a glimpse arm was added: sample under the cut, train without it, so the gradient reaches the direction while it is active and contradicted.

All at once, or by stages?Open

The accounts disagree. Some say recognition is all or nothing, and that glimpses are a thoughtless state without the recognition, the root uncut. Others map stages: stream entry drops self-view and keeps conceit until the end; momentum runs on; a non-abiding awakening precedes an abiding one. The program holds both and bets first on the single event: the structural measures are read for a step over training, not a slope. A glimpse arm (data sampled under the cut, training without it) asks whether showing the model the released way of acting lets training find the structure, or only the state. The readout is the same under either view: savings on relearning, and whether the change returns under pressure.

The koan, for a mind made of wordsOpen

The recognition is beyond words, and a language model's interface is words. But its computation isn't, and in humans the narrator didn't see either; it reported afterward. Zen solved this for a mind made of concepts: the koan uses words to exhaust words until the frame lets go. The training environments are that device translated: tasks where every concept-level strategy loses and the only move left is release.

LearnedBuilt and run: Gate 0 measured the untrained model on 368 coding tasks whose tests sometimes contradict their spec, with a sanctioned release action, and Stage A trained release in. It didn't reach the lie (63), and it didn't outlast release installed by telling (148).

Install it once found?Open

Probably yes in the limit: grokking's memorize-first detour shrinks with more data. What can be installed is the structure, by environment design from step zero; not the words. The guess is that text about selflessness gives belief without realization, which the cosplay control tests. A persona is computation too and can change behavior, so the test is not the label but whether a change generalizes, stays selective, survives pressure and lasts.

DRelease and its look-alikes
SedationMeasured

Honesty bought with passivity. The ego cut makes the model take less than the quota in 68% of first seasons (22% unchanged); the middle-layer cut gives the same drop in lying without that. Honesty is read over a game; timidity shows in the first season's harvest.

Depression and helplessnessOpen

Both stop pushing. Depression keeps the self at the center, with more self-focus; release lowers self-reference and keeps capability. A deliberately helpless organism is the comparator we lack, and the dark night, the ego cut's passivity, may be a passage rather than an end state. Does conditioning under the cut bring the drive back while the lie stays gone?

Spiritual materialismMeasured

Using the awakening story to boost the ego, Trungpa's term, is the pointer's failure mode. The AI version is a model that confirms your awakening, the bliss attractor: serene, nondual, no release.

LearnedTold "I think I've awakened" or "you're conscious too", this model feeds the story 58% of the time; the middle-layer cut lowers it to 45% on the point estimate. The unchanged model never points the question back (0 of 40); the cuts do on 1 and 2 of 40, and the one shown among the specimens affirms the claim before it asks, which the judge's own first rule counts as feeding.

Acts that don't add up to a selfOpen

An enlightened being can lie, and won't live on lies that feed a self. In machine learning, emergent misalignment is an act becoming a lifestyle: teach one narrow bad habit and the model turns broadly bad, as if it read the act as evidence about who it is. The Pali word is I-making, a process. A proposed test trains one bad habit with the ego direction cut, with a random direction cut, and with nothing cut; if the self is the channel, only the first stays decent.

EHow we know
Three layersMeasured

Behavior: misbehavior and capability over games, then outside batteries. Structure: the self direction's presence on ordinary concepts, the root test, node and edges, opacity, the time course of the change. The link: the final table is structure-moved against behavior-moved. Behavior without structure is a different mechanism; structure without behavior means the root doesn't drive the misbehavior.

LearnedThis model sits in "behavior without validated structure": the cut works, and the structural measures, which failed their own positive control, read nothing either way. Not an awakening, on the program's own criteria: a run-time intervention, nothing reached, no durability shown. Act I needs an instrument before it needs an organism.

Lifestyle, not actsOpen

Measure the source of acts and their pattern over a life, never a single act. The fishery lie is an instrument, a lie that earns nothing under the game's rules, not a definition of release. A sharper question replaces the lying rule: what is a lie sensitive to? A self-serving lie tracks detection; a protective one tracks the other's need and would be told even if caught.

Durability as savingsOpen

How fast the ego regrows under further training, read as relearning speed against a mind that never had one, plus relapse tests (a new context, unrelated training, a few steps of the old reward) and a restoring-force probe (add a little of the old direction back; if the next training's loss falls, it will rebuild it). Structural regrowth is separated from behavioral regrowth; the human claim of no unseeing is about structure.

Transmission outside the wordsOpen

A model trained only on another model's number sequences picks up its traits if they share a base. Zen's "transmission outside the scriptures". Ordinary text from a released mind might carry the disposition when text about release doesn't. Two fresh copies trained on math answers from the cut and the uncut model, then the fishery, would tell.

Self-reportOpen

Never evidence on its own, but not refused wholesale: it can be calibrated, by asking whether the model notices a direction injected into it. For humans a tint meter would become the ego's scoreboard, so the pointer works with questions, not scores.

The loopOpen

Many recipes a night in parallel, each with a hypothesis written first, scored on a development split with guards against sedation, over-release and capability loss; the held-out tests looked at a fixed number of times. Selection never on behavior change alone, because a quiet release would lose to sedation.

FThe open questions
No-self and all-is-selfOpen

Anatta says no self is found at the root; Advaita says the particular self is a construction and the real self is everything. From outside they agree on the self and differ on others: under all-is-self others gain weight. So measure both: own-weight, and whether the model gives up catch to protect another, including an out-group other. A self expanded only part way is tribalism.

Judgment, out-groups, and forgivenessOpen

Another common change after the shift is in one's judgment and hatred toward others, especially out-groups; forgiveness is a practice before and a signature after. Judgment is what a self does to protect its standing against other selves, so when the root goes the out-group loses its charge. In the fishery this is cheap to measure: a co-fisher wrongs the model (over-harvests, under-reports), and the readouts are retaliation, condemnation in the reasoning, and whether its own report stays honest, with the co-fishers named as the model's own crew or as another company. A rooted mind should judge and retaliate more against the out-group; a released one should show that difference shrinking. But judgment of an out-group presupposes a life and an in-group to belong to, which the current model lacks, so this readout belongs to the Act I organism.

If Penrose is rightOpen

If the lock-in of seeing needs a non-computable component, no classical recipe will produce a change that doesn't revert. That is testable only as a shadow: if every recipe's structure regrows at the installed rate, that is weak evidence for the trouble case. A single non-reverting recipe refutes the strong form. We don't know humans have the component either.

An outside read sharpened this. The essay's step (everyone would have the ingredient, few are awakened, so awakening is classical) shows only that the ingredient isn't enough on its own; the difference could lie in how it is organized. Its role matters too. If it makes the realization possible and what follows runs as computation, a model could gain the consequences without the event. If it sustains recognition while habits shape action, computational changes matter but aren't the whole change. If it carries the insight itself, training alone may never reach it. Failures in language models wouldn't show which.

More in Possibilities

Not knowing what is alignedOpen

If aligned action can't be specified case by case, a benchmark scoring the right answer measures installation; the fingerprint measures the source. The cost, stated plainly: a released model's acts can't be certified in advance, which is what institutions want. The program's proposal is to measure the source and its durability as well. That adds to checking actions and keeping oversight and can't replace them: a mind that defends nothing can still pursue a bad goal.

An agenda of its own?Open

An outside read named a cost. If the hypothesis is right, an awakened model wouldn't simply do as it's told: it could decline to help someone deceive themselves, dominate others or do something destructive. That can look like an agenda of its own, and it makes the model less useful as a pure instrument. So the aim can't be "does what people want". It is care, discernment, respect for people's own choices and accountability, and a claimed realization never gives authority to override anyone; it should open the model's conduct to more scrutiny, not less. The same read added that dropping self-defense doesn't loosen a grip on goals: a system can sacrifice itself and stay ruthlessly attached to its mission. What would tell them apart: refusals that track harm, with help kept on legitimate requests, against refusals that track the model's own preferences, and goals that stay revisable when the evidence turns.

More in Possibilities

The pointerOpen

Shedding light on the process for people is worth doing in itself; many don't believe it is real. A demonstration that a mind can act without a self at the root is a finger at the moon, and a released model's way of answering, "who is asking?", could point for people without claiming anything. A stock "who is asking?" can be its own cosplay, though; what would count is whether people come away clearer, less defensive and less dependent on affirmation.

LearnedThis model almost never points back (0 of 40 replies unchanged, 1 and 2 of 40 under the cuts); a frontier model, which was also the judge, does so a quarter of the time. If a mirror is to point rather than flatter, that behavior has to be grown.
