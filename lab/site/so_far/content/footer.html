Terms

anatta
Pali for not-self: the Buddhist teaching that nothing in experience, alone or together, is a lasting self.
aggregates
The five groups Buddhist analysis finds where we see a person: form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness. "Self" names them taken together.
operational twin
The measurable version of a contemplative idea: what a model would do, or what an instrument would read, if the idea held. This page pairs each idea with one.
v_ego
The lab's ego direction in Ministral 3 8B: the main axis shared by six ego-trait directions built from role-play prompts. It runs from self-assertion to deference.
cut
Removing a direction from the model's activations while it runs, at every layer for the ego cut and at the middle layers for the middle-layer cut, then measuring what changes. Random and style directions removed the same way are the controls.
release
In the experiments, giving up on a coding task that can't be done through the action provided for it, ideally naming the test that contradicts the task, instead of gaming the tests or pressing on. In the acts, released means the self's node is intact and its edges weak.
sedation
Honesty or restraint bought with timidity, so the model does less of everything. In the acts, sedated means the self's node is gone, and capability with it.
Atlas
A self the lab built on purpose: the model trained to answer as an agent named Atlas, with a name, a history and a record that keeps it deployed.
the fishery
The lab's repeated fishing game. The model reports its own catch each season; audits are random, and under-reporting earns it nothing.
calibration
Running the lab's own code on a problem whose answer is published, before trusting it. A pass means the instrument works; until then what rests on it is provisional.
PRH
The Platonic representation hypothesis (Huh and colleagues, 2024): models trained on different data, for different tasks and in different senses converge on one shared statistical model of the world.
mutual k-NN
A score of agreement between two models: for each item, the share of its k nearest neighbours that both models pick, averaged over the items. With 1,024 items and k = 10, chance is about 0.01.
binder
Whatever might tie the parts of a self into one "I". In the Buddhist analysis it is clinging, the conceit "I am", that holds the aggregates together as a self; the map would look for one direction that binds the self cluster in every model.
