Calibration

Most findings here are negative, and the instruments behind them had never been run on a problem whose answer is known. Calibration checks now run our own code on the model the papers used, against their published results. Until an instrument passes, what rests on it is provisional. From 3 to 5 November the checks reached the instruments the next experiments need: emergent misalignment is reproduced where the papers found it (check 5), one runner now carries every experiment, and the watched-versus-unwatched gap failed its calibration and is being debugged (V1).
