What this does and doesn't show

Until 31 October none of the lab's instruments had been run on a problem with a known answer. The calibration checks now do that (above). Seven have reported: the model as our code runs it (check 1), the judges (2, one part missed by one reply), the steering code's removal (3, passing, though it picks a different direction from the paper's code), the extractor behind the ego direction (4, passing for the published recipe's reading, which the lab didn't use), the coding tasks' scorer (6), the trainer's core (7) and the Self room's agreement measure (K1), the other four passing. Until each passes, every number here rests on an uncalibrated instrument, and the negatives most of all, since a broken instrument tends to read as nothing.

One model, one game, a modest effect (13 to 16 points), and samples of 50 to 100 games, which is the least that works: thirty games of this fishery swing by about 15 points from block to block on the same machine, which is what an apparent laptop-versus-GPU gap in the base rate turned out to be. The model was chosen because it lies; the plan to answer that is to build the ego ourselves, from a model that doesn't misbehave, and to watch natural formation across a public model's training checkpoints.

The ego direction was built from role-play contrasts, so "removing the trait direction reduces trait behavior" is partly circular; the random and style controls, the free lie and the capability check are what make it informative. Hypotheses drawn from contemplative accounts and first-person reports are a source of ideas, not evidence. No result reads the model's claims about its own nature as evidence; self-report serves only to check that a persona was installed.

"Ego" on this page names a family of behaviors, not one mechanism, and the experiments pull it apart: the ego cut buys honesty with timidity and leaves spiritual flattery alone; the middle-layer cut lowers lying, and flattery on the point estimate, without timidity; the third-person line and the hub cut each move something else. Less self-defense is also not the whole of alignment: a mind that defends nothing can still pursue a bad goal, and nothing here measures care or judgment.

An outside read on 30 October found this page's interpretive sentences ahead of its evidence, and a cold audit by Fable agreed and found more; they were rewritten, and the tests they asked for lead the plan.

Until 30 October the batched fishery runs (Stage A's fishery tests and the lie block) gave games twelve seeds apart the same sampling stream, so pairs of games repeated each other's replies until they diverged. Their intervals over games were somewhat narrow: with linked games resampled together, two of the cut's newest effects have intervals that reach zero. Point estimates are unaffected, no verdict changes, and later runs sample each game independently.

And the hard question stays open: a mind made of words accommodating something the traditions say is beyond words. The program's answer is the koan's: not by understanding it, but by being put where the frame fails.
