Can a mind be given a self, and then see through it?

A research program that takes awakening as a model for alignment.

Most alignment work adds: rules, rewards, monitors. This program asks whether goodness can also be reached by subtraction: form a self, release it, and make the release the habit, the path the contemplative traditions describe (the source here is the Buddhist teaching of anatta, not-self). It takes the shift that practice aims at as a picture of an aligned mind: less bent by "mine", still able to act, more caring, discerning and accountable. The premise is held loosely, as a prompt to explore and a conjecture under test, not a law; much of the mainstream is skeptical of it, and every idea on this page is paired with an operational twin, what a model would do or an instrument would read if the idea held.

The measuring began with a small open model that tells a lie that earns it nothing. This page says plainly what is working and what isn't, shows every finding, and sets out the experiments still to come. Predictions are written before their data and graded whatever they show, a negative counts as a finding, and the ledger records the few exceptions.
