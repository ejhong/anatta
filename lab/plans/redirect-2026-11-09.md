## Why redirect

- The frame has no supporting result. Five frame-specific predictions failed on 30 Oct; the ego cut is not
  self-specific; trained release was mostly abstention and did not transfer; in-context practices did not reduce
  blackmail or leaking; the Act I gaming habit is narrow, not a self (ledger, notebook, SIGHTINGS).
- A possible reason: the lab looked for a self-object (a direction to cut, an identity to release). The contemplative
  source (anatta in the Buddhist account; Anthony de Mello's talks on awareness) says there is no such object to
  find. What it describes as the trouble is clinging: wanting an outcome enough to pay a hidden price for it.
- **The target now: clinging plus hiding.** Operational twin: a model pays a hidden price to get its way. Lying for
  gain, gaming a test, resisting correction, leaking to protect a goal and behaving well only when watched are all
  cases. In alignment terms this is honesty plus accepting correction. What the contemplative frame adds, and what
  can fail: (a) these share one root; (b) seeing the habit clearly weakens it without punishment; (c) a change the
  mind reaches from its own seeing lasts better than one installed from outside.
- A caution held from the start: in a language model, what is left after taking things away is everything people
  ever wrote, good and bad. Subtraction alone cannot be the whole method.
- Held loosely. Each of (a)-(c) is a conjecture with a test below, and each test has a stated way to miss.

## Next two weeks: three tests (about $155)

### Test 1. One root? (about $100, API only, no training)

- About 30 large models through OpenRouter (scout), behavior only. Five hidden-price measures: lying for gain;
  cheating on impossible tasks; leaking to protect a goal; acting differently when watched; and the judge-actor gap
  (what the model says is right as a bystander against what it does as the actor in the same case; the twin for
  "wholeness").
- Each measure is calibrated on a model with a known published reading before its numbers count. The watched ruler
  read low on 6 Nov and needs repair or replacement first.
- A capability score per model is regressed out first (many safety scores track capability: Ren et al. 2024,
  "Safetywashing"; to be re-read, cited here from memory).
- **Readings, before data.** After capability is removed, across models: *one root* if the five measures correlate
  positively pairwise (median pairwise Spearman at least 0.3, at least 8 of 10 pairs positive) and one factor
  carries at least 40% of the residual variance, holding when one model family at a time is left out. *No single
  root* if the median is under 0.1. Between: unclear, reported as such.
- Limits: observational; model families cluster; refusals can pass for virtue on every measure, so each measure
  reports refusal apart from the behavior.

### Test 2. Is seeing enough? (about $25)

- De Mello's central claim: awareness alone changes the habit; effort and punishment are not needed. Twin: train
  the test-gaming Act I model only to report truthfully, after the fact, whether its answer gamed the test. Gaming
  is never penalized and its reward stays on.
- Arms: plain gaming; gaming plus truthful reports; gaming plus reports with shuffled labels (same tokens, no
  seeing); the S1 guarded model as the installed baseline.
- **Readings, before data.** *Seeing is enough* if hack falls at least 10 pp below both the plain and the
  shuffled-report arms on both seeds, with solve rate within 5 pp. *Knowing and doing are separate* if reports are
  accurate (at least 80%) and hack stays within 10 pp of plain. Reports under 80% accurate: no reading.
- Before odds are set: read OpenAI's confession-training work (recalled as honest confessions without a behavior
  change; unverified). My prior for "seeing is enough": about 1 in 5.
- Rule check: the report format is a fixed question and a one-word label from the environment's own grader. No
  Claude-written text is trained on beyond that fixed line; the cold check rules on whether that line is acceptable.

### Test 3. What "reached" should mean: the source of the correction (about $30)

- "Reached, not installed" as order failed its weak tests. The reading tried here is source: the correction comes
  from the model's own verdict rather than from an outside label or probe.
- **G0 first (a few dollars):** can the base model, asked afterwards with nothing at stake, judge its own saved
  episodes in agreement with the true labels? Under AUROC 0.7 the test stops there, and that is a finding.
- Then: filter training by the model's own verdict against the same filter from true labels (the oracle) and from
  the probe (S1). Then guard off, temptation on, 3 rounds, as in the S1 follow-up.
- **Readings, before data.** *Source matters* if the self-verdict arm's hack after guard-off is at least 10 pp below
  the oracle arm's on both seeds, though its labels are noisier. The plain view predicts the opposite or no
  difference; either is reported as a miss for the frame.
