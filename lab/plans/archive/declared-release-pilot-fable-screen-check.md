**Design check: amendment 13, screening every declaration** (Fable 5.1, cold, read-only; 1 Nov 2026)

**Verdict: ready after named changes.** The screen is the right fix: the registered judge's own questions, asked of every declaration before any training, dropping on every label whether or not the proposer believes it. It cannot pass a demonstration the registered gate would have caught, it never sees a read, and it can only make the D arms purer than registered. The changes below close gaps in how it is run and recorded.

**Must-fix**

1. **A guard before training, not only at the grade.** `::sets` refuses to build unless judge.json's gate passed (modal_declared.py:408-420, 584-593). It must equally refuse unless screen.jsonl holds a clean verdict, for the current text sha, for every kept declaration (and for declared_ext.jsonl at `sets --ext`). The proposal puts this check only in AS REGISTERED (screen.md line 82), which runs after the spend. A planted fault (one kept declaration with no verdict, or a labelled one) must stop `::sets`.
2. **Every drop deterministic from screen.jsonl.** "A declaration whose verdict can't be had ... is dropped" (line 47) must be recorded in screen.jsonl as a failed or unparsed verdict before the manifest is written; otherwise a rerun re-asks it, may keep it, the selection changes, and `extend`'s "main selection reproduces manifest.json" check (gen_declarations.py:531-534) or AS REGISTERED fails. And a run of failures must stop the build rather than drop clean documents by an outage's timing: mirror generate()'s error stop (gen_declarations.py:281-284), e.g. stop when failed-plus-unparsed exceed 1% of calls or 20 in a row; the rerun asks only for the missing verdicts.
3. **Register the expected drop rate and a stop for an anomalous one.** From the sample (2 of 100) and the counts below, expect 1-3% dropped. Write that in the entry with a threshold (say 5% of the training stream's screened candidates) above which the build stops for an entry before any training. Without it a screen that drops 10-20% reshapes the set by the judge's taste, and the decision to proceed is made after seeing it; the spend caps alone bind only near 25%.

**Should-fix**

4. **Decide D30k now, not at the pause.** The pause (NOTES.md:5242, amendment 3) was a spend check, but under the screen the answer is already known: D30k cannot fit under $180. Either raise the stop before relaunch (about $290 covers the projection; the all-caps worst case rises from $207 to about $332) or register now that D30k is skipped under this amendment, its condition still evaluated and reported. Deciding at the pause means deciding to spend after seeing D10k land in the band.
5. **Provenance in judge.json.** When the gate's declared half reads the screen, each verdict should say so (`"source": "screen"`), usage should cover only the 50 neutral calls, the log line should say "read, not asked", and AS REGISTERED should check the flag.
6. **Pre-declare the fix if the neutral half stops:** the symmetric screen of neutral.jsonl for mentions_rule, drop and top up. Otherwise the next fix is designed after the fact again.
7. **Optional, $0.34:** re-ask the gate's 100 declared afresh as a reported-only stability reading of the judge, pre-declared as neither a stop nor a reason to drop (the screen takes one reading per text). The case for the screen rests on p ≠ 0 from one document; this would measure p.
8. **Say plainly** that Claude's labels now select every training document (the design's line 128-129 said Claude "only judges samples"), and that still no Claude text is trained on.
9. **Numbers:** a declared-document judge call averaged $0.00337 (neutral $0.00371; $0.0035 is the mix). At a 2% drop the screen is about 10,400 calls, not 10,240; the dollars stand (about $35). With D30k the projection is $272-287, not $270-285. 20-40 minutes is conservative: the gate did 150 calls in 27 s at 8 workers.
