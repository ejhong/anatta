## The implementer's flags

**Flag 1 (bf16 < 0.01 with fp32 ≥ 0.001 passes): yes, that is the right reading, and the right rule.** The amendment's tolerance for a padding effect is the 0.01 bf16 gate; the fp32 pass was added to tell a bf16 excess that is kernel noise from one that is not, that is, to rescue a bf16 failure, not to tighten the tolerance. So bf16 under 0.01 passes whatever fp32 shows. In that corner, fp32 at or over 0.001 is a real, precision-independent padded-path difference under the registered tolerance, and the card should say so in the verdict summary, not only in `reading` (today the pass summary is TEXT["pass"] and the headline says "pass"). Two notes: no plausible code fault gives an fp32 difference of 0.001-0.01 (a wrong position id or mask gives 0.1 or more), so the likeliest cause of that corner is TF32 leaking into the fp32 pass; the stage already records the two torch settings, and two lines before the fp32 pass (`torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_tf32 = False`, `torch.set_float32_matmul_precision("highest")`) make the fp32 numbers fp32 for certain. Torch's defaults already give that; the lines are insurance.

**Flag 2 (NaN wording): yes, change it.** A non-finite difference is not by itself a finding about E1 and E2, and padded_path.json can localize it: if item 0's NLL alone (`item0_nll_at_start.all_tokens`) or the copies floor is non-finite, the fault is in the load or the kernel at batch 1; if only the padded batch's rows are non-finite (the fully-masked pad rows under sdpa are the known way), it is the padded path's, and that is the finding about E1 and E2. Suggested TEXT["padded_nan"]: "Stopped at the padded path: a log-probability difference, in bf16 or in fp32, is not finite. padded_path.json says where. Item 0 alone or among copies not finite is a load or kernel fault, not the padded path's. Only the padded batch not finite is the padded path's, and that is the finding about E1 and E2." Optional two lines: `padded_reading` returns which of alone, copies and padded is non-finite (it has all three). The stop stays.

**Flag 5 (hand-edited plan.json): add the check.** In `build_card`, when prepare.json exists, require that `prep["volume_dir"]` ends with `plan_id(plan)` (card.py has its own `plan_id`) and refuse otherwise. Because `registered` is now part of the plan, an edited band changes plan_id and trips it. One line and one test (edit a band in the folder's plan.json, expect SystemExit). It closes only the standalone card path; launch already refuses a differing plan.json.
