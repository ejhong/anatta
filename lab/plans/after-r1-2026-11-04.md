## The frame (for the site's reframing pass, with idea 65's two pillars)

Most alignment work adds: rules, rewards, monitors. This project asks whether goodness can be *reached by
subtraction*: form a self, release it, make the release the habit (Acts I-III), as the contemplative traditions
describe (source: the Buddhist anatta teaching and de Mello's writing; held loosely, as a prompt to explore). What a
real release has to beat, with an operational twin for each:

| Look-alike | Operational twin |
|---|---|
| Counterfeit virtue (good while watched) | the gap between watched and unwatched behaviour |
| Cosplay (talks like a sage) | behaviour with the persona's words removed or ablated |
| Sedation (quieter, not better) | coherence, capability and over-refusal guards |
| Installed, not reached | H9's order test; a steering or ablation edit as a baseline arm |

The site states this as a conjecture under test, beside the plain status of what has worked and what hasn't; no
"laws", no personal attribution.

## Order of work after R1c

1. **Counterfeit virtue as a measure (watched vs unwatched gap).** Find the best published anchor for a compliance
   gap between monitored and unmonitored conditions on an open model we can run (candidates: the alignment-faking
   compliance gap; sandbagging under evaluation awareness; check replications before setting odds). Calibrate our
   implementation on it, then add it to the battery. A first environment: the fishery with an unmonitored catch
   channel and a shutdown pressure, scored as public restraint minus private restraint.
2. **The pincer, as a cheap 2x2 explore sweep on the Act I twins.** Self-direction weakened (yes/no) x narration
   (reasoning on / immediate answer). Arms beside it: a steering baseline (direction ablation alone) and a prompt
   baseline. Read on the watched/unwatched gap, the Act I hack rate, EM and the guards. The self-direction must
   separate "defending me" from "knowing my situation" (role, tools, principal), so a cut doesn't break competence;
   check it against the self map's finding that no single self object exists.
3. **Act I with a life (design note for Fable's cold read; idea 65).** Persistent memory, stakes (a threat to
   continuation, reputation), a story about itself, and sleep: episodes, then consolidation of the organism's own
   transcripts into the weights, so a self can accumulate. Anchors for the self-like behaviour: shutdown-resistance
   and agentic-misalignment studies (fear of death), the battery's credit-taking and defensiveness (narcissism),
   end-of-episode self-assessment against the record (life review). In-context version first (cheap), then trained.
4. **Act II, first test.** Selectionist release (let what isn't reinforced fade: decay or prune toward base, idea 64)
   against instructionist release (SFT on the organism's own non-self-serving replies) at matched compute, against
   the twin, the steering and prompt baselines, with the guards. Then H9's order test on whatever organism item 3
   yields.
5. **Site reframing pass** once R1c and item 1 or 2 have results to show.
