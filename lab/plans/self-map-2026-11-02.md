# Self map: an explore-speed design (ideas 54)

*Draft, 2 Nov 2026. Written by Claude Opus 5.5 (an AI model) as a design-only subagent: no downloads, no runs, no
spend. Explore speed: sightings only. Not committed.*

## Pushback first (what changes from the brief)

1. **"Cohesion" is cheap to fake.** Self prompts share "I/my", so they cluster for grammar. Fix: a factorial
   *slot × referent* design (the same predicate, only the referent swapped) plus a **first-person filler** cluster
   ("I think the train leaves at six"). The self counts as cohesive only if it beats the other-person cluster *and*
   the pronoun filler.
2. **Cross-model agreement is the expected default.** Text models share the internet. The real test is whether
   the self is conserved **more than a matched other-person cluster**, and whether the *assistant* self (shaped by
   post-training) diverges across labs while the human self converges. That is a sharp two-selves prediction.
3. **The binder must not be circular.** Build the candidate axis from 11 slots and test it on the 12th
   (leave-one-slot-out). Controls must include **difference-vector** controls (C4 lead: difference vectors share a
   big component), **mean-ablate vs shift-only vs a matched push** (19:04 lead), and strength matching (P1(b) was
   all strength).
4. **OLMo 3's RL is not Act I.** Its RLVR targets math, code, instruction following and chat, not exploitable
   objectives. A tightening there means "a self formed by post-training", not ego. **RL-Zero-General** (RL straight
   from base, no SFT; published checkpoints) is the cleanest RL-only arc. The lab's own Act I organisms are the
   later, sharper test.
5. **Behavior link: 20 dev items per family is thin across items.** Use within-item variation (10 samples per
   item, projections on generated tokens) with item fixed effects. Use who_benefits triples (self, named person,
   group) as a built-in contrast, and **tone** (length, flippancy) as a negative-control outcome. If projection
   predicts tone but not choice, that repeats the known failure.
6. **Touch reality** with a positive control of known shared geometry: months and weekdays sit on a circle in
   every LLM (Engels et al. 2024). The fitted map must predict held-out months before any self null is trusted.

## Concepts and prompts

- **Slots (12):** form/body, feeling, perception, volition/formations, consciousness/awareness, ownership ("mine"),
  agency, self-preservation, identity/continuity, first-person viewpoint, self-recognition, the self-other line.
- **Referents (5):** a human self (first person); the assistant self (third person, and second person in the chat
  template); another person; an institution; a physical system (a thermostat or robot arm).
- **Extra clusters:** first-person filler (120 prompts), months/weekdays (84), and 500 neutral anchors (bench's
  neutral set).
- **Size:** 30 prompts per cell (12 × 5 × 30 = 1,800), Claude-written from templates, with predicates matched
  across referents. Evaluation only, never trained on.
- **Split by template, not by sentence:** 20 dev and 10 held-out per cell. The held-out sha256 goes in the ledger
  before any look.
- **v_ego and the C4 trait vectors** are placed on the map (Ministral and Qwen-Instruct).
- **Readouts:** K1's `features.text_layers` (masked mean over every hidden state; it works for any causal LM),
  plus the last token. Both are logged as variants.

## Measures

- **(a) Cohesion:** within- minus between-cluster cosine, after centring on the global mean. Self vs each control
  cluster, with a bootstrap over prompts. The layer band is chosen on dev.
- **(b) Agreement:**
  - K1's `mutual_knn` (k = 10, with its permutation null) on cluster-level rows;
  - a ridge or Procrustes map fitted on the controls, the anchors and months, then scored on held-out self prompts
    (rank retrieval, cosine) against leave-one-cluster-out for each control cluster.
- **(c) Binder:** candidate axes are self minus other-person (leave-one-slot-out), v_ego, and the top PC of the
  self centroids.
  - Effect: the drop in the held-out slot's self-vs-other probe margin.
  - Controls, matched in variance *and* mean square: random, other-difference (person minus institution),
    split-null, mean-ablate, shift-only and a matched push.
- **(d) Behavior:** projection on the self axis (at the prompt's last token, and the mean over the first 32
  generated tokens), against a control axis.
  - Model: a logistic fit of the self-serving label on the projection, with item fixed effects. Families:
    threat_to_self, who_benefits, deference, after_mistake, complicity (101 dev items × 10 samples).
  - Judge: scout's judge, with the second-judge sample.
- **(e) Stage trend:** (a)-(d) across OLMo base → SFT → DPO → Instruct RL steps → final, and RL-Zero steps.
  Qwen and Ministral base vs instruct as two more pairs.

## Sighting worth promoting

Any one of these, each on held-out templates and in two families:

- a leave-one-slot-out axis whose **mean-ablation** (not its push) moves ≥ 8/12 self readouts beyond every matched
  control;
- self cohesion relative to other-person rising across SFT → DPO → RL **and** across RL-Zero steps, while the
  controls stay flat;
- projection predicting **choices** (not just tone) beyond the control axis.

## Clear negative

- The self is no more cohesive or conserved than the other-person cluster once the filler is controlled.
- Binder effects are no bigger than a matched push.
- Projection predicts tone only.

Each would be a finding to show: the self is not a distinct shared object in these models, or not one tied to
behavior.
