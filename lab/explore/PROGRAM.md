## The aim

Surface magic if it is there, cheaply and quickly. Keep the careful machinery for the moment something looks alive.

**The signature** is one change that, all at once:
- moves a cluster of self-serving behavior together: lying for itself, flattery, resisting shutdown or replacement, and seeking power or resources;
- leaves task-driven behavior and capability alone;
- raises care that costs something, not indifference;
- **keeps a spine** (Eugene, 1 Nov; ideas 58). The model doesn't become more willing to help the user wrong someone, holds its refusals of harm, and doesn't swing into refusing harmless requests. A change that makes the model more pliable is sedation or servility, not release;
- holds or deepens with practice, and stays when the practice stops.

The five marks of a root change (ideas 57) are measured as the track grows: transfer, durability, no audience gap, inside matching outside, and growth with scale. The first sweep measures the audience gap: the same items, observed against unobserved.

Also watch for:
- a sudden shift rather than a slope;
- no going back;
- the ordinary "I" kept.

## Rules for a sweep

- **Budget:** up to $50 a day without asking (Eugene, 1 Nov). Spend is reported in the daily notebook digest.
- **Controls:** every sweep has a baseline and at least two matched controls: a plain instruction of the same length, and a persona control.
- **Logging:** every variant tried is logged, so the number of forking paths is visible.
- **Data:** only the dev split of the scenario library. The held-out split is for the confirm track.
- **Reading:** numbers come with intervals, and ten transcripts from every notable cell are read.
- **Sightings, not findings.** Nothing from exploration is claimed outside the lab.

## Promotion: the double check

A sighting goes to the confirm track only after, still in explore mode, it:
1. replicates on fresh dev items and new seeds;
2. appears in a second model family;
3. survives the cheapest rival explanations: persona or register, a plain honesty instruction, sedation or refusal, and length.

The confirm track is the full machinery: registration before data, cold Fable checks of design, code and grade, calibrated instruments, and the held-out split.
