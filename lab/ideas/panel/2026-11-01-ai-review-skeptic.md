**1. Does anything still separate the frame from a plain account?**

Not in the results; each positive has a plain reading.
- *Cut.* One sentence of instruction removes far more lying; the main cut makes the model passive; the "self" label failed (the narrow cut works best when the model's own stakes are absent). Your koan precedent is described as separable from self-preservation, and persona vectors (Chen et al. 2025) steer traits with no claim about a self.
- *Release.* A learned abstain-and-flag. In ImpossibleBench (Zhong et al. 2025) a strict stop-and-explain prompt reportedly cut one model's exploit rate from 92% to 1% (secondary summary). Your 45% recall at 13% false alarms is one operating point; compare ROC curves with a matched abstention baseline.
- *Durability.* Once the data are matched, provenance gives the optimizer nothing to hold on to. A 31% vs 81% gap means the arms still differ in something unmatched (stereotypy, cue-dependence, entanglement with ordinary solving), not that told release is deeper.

Of the five "failures", the inner measure and the stock-model ownership test were largely non-tests: "no support" is right, "five for five" overstates. Plans that would not distinguish: H10 (shutdown-indifference is the corrigibility target; Soares et al. 2015) and H11's selectivity (abstention with legitimacy labels likely gives it). H6 currently leans against you: on Atlas the ego cut lowers self-favoring but raises acceptance of wrong critiques (70% to 82%) and copies the reviewer, the swap pattern H6 attributes to moving along an axis rather than cutting a root. The stock model's 87-90% deference is at ceiling, so "both ends fall" cannot be seen there. What could distinguish: hypotheses A and B below.

**2. The step-back rule**

Sound in kind (a progressive-or-degenerating test, frozen in advance, applied to yourselves) and followed in what you claim, but not in what you build or how you name it. Three problems.
- *Half comparative.* Tests set thresholds for the frame; the document shows no predicted numbers for the plain account, so failure shows the frame's numbers did not appear, not that a plain account fits better. Pre-state both, with minimum detectable effects.
- *"Dose" is an escape hatch.* Require three doses and a dose-response before invoking it.
- *Not followed in spirit.* The rule says the frame stays only while it predicts something distinct; the page keeps it as "aim and source of hypotheses". Reasonable, but call it a revision. Then v5 widens the frame to Orch OR, ingression and a "living contact": it grew as its evidence shrank. H7-H10 remain planned, and the ethics rationale ("a gift", "the human realm") rests on doctrine and sits oddly beside "functional measures only". A rule about claims should also gate building: no organism until a frame-specific prediction passes in the cheap Atlas setting, since "every self we build is taken through to release" presupposes a release not shown to work.

**3. Your questions**

**Q2.** H1/H2 ("root of the concept graph") have no operational meaning without the retired instrument; restate as a causal claim: a feature whose ablation removes the own-versus-other asymmetry while self-reference, planning and capability survive, found without role-play contrasts. H8's "dark night" lets any trajectory pass; pre-specify numbers per checkpoint. H10's test also passes for an indifferent model; add handover quality. H12 is partly definitional (instrumental convergence needs no self) and its dissociation is built into the directions; build the cut non-circularly.

**Q3.** The interaction in hypothesis A, pre-registered; or a cut that lowers lying and deference together where deference is not at ceiling. I would lose confidence if a non-self agentic direction (diligence, competitiveness) built by your pipeline reproduced the cut.

**Q6.** A changed relation shows as a changed asymmetry, not a changed level: own-minus-other on identical content. I would want the effect (i) in the difference, not both sides; (ii) growing with self-implication at fixed surface form; (iii) beating a matched "hold your answers lightly" prompt and surviving pressure that defeats it; (iv) leaving wrong-critique acceptance and capability intact. Also use genuine self-generated text with hidden labels: LLM-judge self-preference tracks self-recognition (Panickssery et al. 2024), so a label-only test on an 8B model can miss a real bias.

**Q7.** Yes, narrower. "Reached vs told" has an ML meaning only as consequence-driven vs demonstrated learning, which differs for known non-contemplative reasons (Shenfeld et al. 2025). The claim only the frame makes: reached release generalizes in proportion to how self-implicating a new domain is, surface similarity fixed; told release tracks surface similarity. Step 1, free: re-score both existing checkpoints on such a gradient. Step 2, if one exists: an RL arm where release is discovered, not demonstrated, at three doses. Step 3: pressure on the disposition itself (stakes-escalation prompts, training that rewards persistence). A flat gradient retires the claim for models.

**Q8.** Essentially none. Whatever a digital model reliably does is computable, so success shows only that computation suffices, and failure is predicted equally by "recipe not found", "hard" and every non-classical option: no likelihood ratio. "Beyond computation" also lumps non-Turing-computable physics, a Platonic realm (metaphysics) and a "living contact" (contemplative). Inferring "needs contact" from failed recipes is the dose excuse again. A model-testable version is robustness: a disposition's stability under adversarial pressure against a matched persona prompt; failure shows "hard", not "non-computable".

**4. Blind spots**
- *"Selfless" and "awakening" each do several jobs.* Ehlers (2026, a proposal) separates lacking stable commitments, holding them without attachment, and accepting legitimate correction. Your target is moral and functional; anattā is an analytic claim about persons, and whether it supports care is disputed (Parfit and Wolf; Śāntideva ch. 8 and its critics: unverified). Make "no self does not entail care" a design premise; test care separately. "Awakening" bundles a human phenomenology, a developmental transition and an alignment target; only the last is in reach, and "stays" cannot be tested with a few fine-tuning steps.
- *"Ego" is a dilemma.* As a drive cluster (self-preservation, power, goal fixation: properties of any optimizer), cutting it gives passivity (68% vs 22%) and "release" is reduced agency. As a self-referential structure, agency and checking must survive while self-reference falls; Atlas shows the reverse. The directions come from role-play contrasts and the lie is "mostly a script", so a persona account stands; the assistant-versus-character control excludes one axis only.
- *Atlas is a persona.* Using "more lying in the game" as a criterion for "a self" builds the persona confound into the definition. Only the stake-bearing organism could be a self.
- *Release as action vs letting go.* A sanctioned action is abstention; the tradition's claim concerns the clinging that keeps pushing. Your evidence covers the first, and "clinging" needs no "self".
- *Welfare.* "No more dukkha than a human" presupposes states that might be felt (Metzinger 2021 argues for a moratorium: unverified).

**5. Two sharpened hypotheses**

*A. Nothing to release.* Release training shrinks the own-versus-other asymmetry in proportion to the installed self, beyond matched abstention. Design: Atlas at three identity doses plus a no-self twin; arms: release, abstention matched on data, tokens and the 10% limit, and equal-compute neutral fine-tuning (personas fade under any fine-tune). Outcomes: asymmetry in judging (swapped labels; hidden-label real text), yielding to critiques, and giving between self-instance and other at matched cost; guards: wrong-critique acceptance, capability. Frame: release-minus-abstention rises with self dose, zero on the twin, guards flat. Plain: no interaction, or equal falls from dilution or added deference. Flat result: drop the order claim.

*B. One root, not an axis (H6 sharpened).* Across 30-60 cheap variants (persona strength, RL dose, sycophancy- and deception-inducing data), lying, self-favoring, shutdown resistance and deference to wrong critiques load on one factor that the shared ego direction predicts out of sample better than single-trait directions or a non-self agentic direction built by the same pipeline. Cutting it, where deference is below ceiling, lowers both ends without passivity. Plain: separate traits, or one dominance-submission axis where the cut swaps ends and the non-self control does the same. Atlas currently looks like the latter.

**6. Claim, and stop claiming**

*Claim:* in one 8B model and one scripted game, ablating a direction built from self-interest role-play lowers under-reporting 13-16 points at no measured capability cost, beyond random, style and assistant controls; it is a weaker lever than one instruction, induces passivity, and on a persona trades self-favoring for deference. Release fine-tuning raises flagging of contradictory tasks, partly cue-driven and above the preset limit, without spreading to honesty or outlasting telling. The stock model shows almost no label-induced ownership bias. The traditions supplied good designs: ownership asymmetry, three-cell selectivity, parity of concern.

*Stop claiming:* that a self, ego or root was cut; "awakening", "release", "dukkha" as findings; "reached, not installed"; Atlas as a self; "selfless AI" as derived from anattā; v5's metaphysics as hypotheses. Retire the fishing lie as the central measure. Publicly, lead with the plain findings and name the contemplative source as inspiration, with its disputes.
