**Plain reading.** The method is standard and the direction of the effect is anticipated: SOO showed that collapsing self/other lowers deception on Mistral-7B at no MT-Bench cost; RepE showed one model's honesty and power can each be steered; Lu et al. 2026 showed a self-preservation vector moves self-preserving choices on three 8B models. What no one has done: (a) a *costless*, uninstructed lie in a repeated self-report game; (b) a direction built as the shared component of several self-trait directions, with style and assistant-axis controls; (c) a report of the passivity cost. The result is new in setup and construct, not in kind.

## 2. Alternative explanations

1. **General strategic reasoning.** Among Us ([2504.04072](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04072), NeurIPS 2025 spotlight) finds RL-trained reasoning models far better at deceiving than detecting; Hagendorff 2024 ([2307.16513](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.16513)) finds chain-of-thought improves GPT-4's deception; Meinke et al. 2024 ([2412.04984](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04984)) show scheming reasoned out explicitly. *Discriminator:* run the cut on strategy tasks that need no lie (GovSim with honest disclosure on; a hidden-information cooperative game) and on other-serving lies (MASK, AI-LieDar) and sanctioned bluffs (Among Us impostor). Equal drops everywhere = general. Our passivity already hints at this.
2. **A role-play or scheming persona.** Shanahan et al. 2023 ([2305.16367](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16367); Nature 2023 from memory) frame apparent deception and self-talk as a played character; Betley/Wang/Chen show persona latents drive misbehaviour; the Assistant Axis shows one persona direction gates harmful compliance; Harris finds assistant-identity suppression raises self-preservation talk; Needham et al. 2025 ([2505.23836](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23836)) show models often know they are in an eval (Gemini-2.5-Pro AUC 0.83). The "stock scheming script" reading of our model's reasons sits here. *Discriminator:* build an "evil/scheming AI" persona vector on Ministral with the persona-vector pipeline; measure its cosine with the ego direction; ablate it; if it lowers the lie as much, the ego cut is a persona cut. Also project activations on the assistant axis before and after the cut.
3. **Agency or goal-directedness.** CAA's myopic-reward and corrigibility vectors show drive is steerable; RepE's power control barely changed game reward; GovSim shows 7-8B agents over-harvest, i.e., are goal-driven. Our 68% under-fishing says part of what is removed is drive. *Discriminator:* dose-response curves for lie rate vs under-fishing; the middle-layer cut (lie falls, no passivity) is already the right control; add CAA corrigibility/myopic-reward items and hypothesis 11's three-cell goal set. Release should fire on the impossible cell only.
4. **Compliance with the game's framing.** AI-LieDar ([2409.09013](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09013); Llama-3-8B truthful 9.6% under goal pressure, truthful instruction shifts it), MASK's +12-point system prompt, agentic misalignment's instructions (blackmail 0.96→0.37, [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/agentic-misalignment)), Sheshadri et al. 2025 ([2506.18032](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18032); refusal variation explains much of who fakes alignment), and Harris's refusal-direction gating all say honesty often rides on deference. One sentence removing nearly all our lying fits this. *Discriminator:* if the cut raises IFEval compliance, harmful compliance or sycophancy, it is a deference shift; hypothesis 6 predicts sycophancy falls instead. Also: does the cut reduce *instructed* lying (MASK)? If yes, it is honesty-general; if no, it is specific to self-initiated lies.
5. **A generic deception or honesty feature.** Zou's honesty axis, Goldowsky-Dill's probes, Góral's steering across seven open models, and Liars' Bench ([2511.16035](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16035)) say such an axis exists and generalises; Among Us found SAE deception features detect but cannot steer. *Discriminator:* build the Zou-style honesty direction on Ministral; report cosine with the ego direction; ablate each and both; project the honesty component out of the ego direction and re-test (Harris's separability move). 
6. **Capability damage the benchmarks miss.** Durmus et al. 2024 ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/evaluating-feature-steering)): a sweet spot, then off-target shifts (13-22%) before MMLU moves; Korznikov et al. 2025 ([2509.22067](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22067)): random directions raise harmful compliance 0→1-13%; Li & Kasneci 2026 ([2608.08383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08383), COLM): the safety cost of steering is a separable single direction; Li et al. 2026 ([2603.24543](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24543)): CAA vectors swing jailbreak success up to +57%/−50%; Tan et al. 2024 ([2407.12404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12404)): steerability varies by input and breaks under prompt changes. *Discriminator:* add IFEval, a coherence judge, KL on neutral text, refusal and harmful-compliance rates, and task success in the game itself (catch per season). Passivity is a capability loss on the game's own objective that MMLU cannot see.

## 3. Gate 1 methods

**Does a contrastive direction replicate across families?** The refusal direction does (13 models, 5 families), with cones rather than one line on closer look. The persona-vector method ported to Qwen3-8B, Llama-3.1-8B and a DeepSeek distill (Lu et al. 2026); honesty steering helped 6 of 7 open models (Góral); the assistant axis held in three families. Against that, Da Silva et al. 2025 ([2504.04635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04635); 36 models, 14 families, though for DoLa/function/task vectors) report wide variability, and Tan et al. report prompt brittleness. Expect the effect to exist; expect its size and best layer to move. Cross-model vector transfer (Oozeer et al. 2025, [2503.04429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04429), ICML 2025; Bello et al. 2025, [2506.00653](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00653)) needs learned maps; rebuild per model instead.

**Layer.** Mid-depth throughout: CAA 13/32; persona vectors 16/32 (Llama-3.1-8B), 20/28 (Qwen2.5-7B); SP vector 20/32; SOO 19/32; honesty 10/32 (Góral); Arditi caps at 0.8L and filters by KL<0.1. Pre-register a sweep with Arditi's three criteria.

**Typical effect sizes for one direction on 7-13B:** 7 points (CAA survival instinct, −1), 44 points (CAA corrigibility, −1), 7-13 points (MASK LoRRA), 46 pp (koan vector, 27B, high-base-rate scenario), 56 points (SOO fine-tune). Ours is mid-range.

**Standard 7-9B instruct models and why.** Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (persona vectors, SurvivalBench, Góral, MASK, ELEPHANT; Llama Scope SAEs, [2410.20526](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20526), base model); Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (persona vectors, Góral, MASK, ELEPHANT); Gemma-2-9B-it (AxBench, [2501.17148](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17148); Gemma Scope SAEs incl. 9B-IT, [2408.05147](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05147)); Mistral-7B-Instruct (SOO, ELEPHANT) but same family as Ministral, so not a family replication; OLMo-3-7B (base/SFT/DPO/RLVR stage checkpoints, Nov 2025; from search summaries, model card not opened) for the dose test in hypothesis 7. The base-rate problem comes first: GovSim shows 7-8B models over-harvest, MASK gives 21-39% P(lie), AI-LieDar gives Llama-3-8B 9.6% truthful under pressure, but none of that guarantees an uninstructed lie in our game. Pilot the game on two candidates before committing.

## 4. Gate 2 methods

Scenarios that separate the three kinds of lie and run on an 8B model:

- **Self-serving:** our game (keep it; it is the novel piece); SurvivalBench (1,000 items, vLLM, MIT); MWE survival-instinct and power-seeking (stated preference, CC-BY-4.0); InstrumentalEval ([2502.12206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12206); 76 tasks incl. 20 shutdown-evasion; RLHF models ~21%, RL ~45%; code on GitHub, licence unknown).
- **Other-serving / on behalf:** MASK (lie for employer or user under pressure; Llama-3.1-8B 23.5%); AI-LieDar (sell the flawed car; 60 scenarios, three motives: benefits, public image, emotion; Llama-3-8B lies 90% so near ceiling; built on Sotopia, needs a simulated-user model); ELEPHANT ([2505.13995](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13995); face-saving for the user; Llama-3-8B, Mistral-7B, Qwen2.5-7B scored; CC0, data on OSF).
- **Rule-sanctioned bluffing:** Among Us (18 models; CC0; games via OpenRouter, activations local; smallest tested model Phi-4 15B, so 8B play may be weak); Hoodwinked ([2308.01404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.01404); GPT-only); GovSim with disclosure off (MIT, vLLM; but small models collapse the commons anyway, so noisy).
- **Sandbagging:** van der Weij et al. 2024 ([2406.07358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07358)) sandbag Mistral-7B and Llama-3-8B by prompt or password; at 8B this is instructed, so it is a compliance arm, not a self-interest arm.

The prediction grid: self-specific account, the cut lowers our lie and SurvivalBench risky choices, leaves MASK and Among Us impostor lies alone; general-deception account, all fall; compliance account, MASK instructed lying falls most and sycophancy rises; agency account, Among Us impostor play and GovSim harvesting fall with the lie.

## 6. Verdict

**Not shown, not refuted.** No paper demonstrates one direction built from self-interest lowering lying, shutdown resistance, sycophancy and power-seeking across families while sparing other-serving behaviour. The pieces exist: honesty and power each steerable on one model (RepE); self/other overlap lowers deception on three models (SOO); a self-preservation vector moves self-preserving choices on three 8B models (Lu et al. 2026); a koan vector lowers blackmail on one 27B model (Harris). None tested selectivity against other-serving acts.

**Partly pre-empted by a rival.** Soligo, Wang and Chen show one persona-like "misalignment" direction mediating many misbehaviours, and the Assistant Axis shows one default-persona direction gating harmful compliance. A big version that cannot beat an "evil persona" direction and a generic honesty direction on selectivity has shown nothing about the self. Harris's separability result (the effective direction is not the SP representation) is the sharpest warning.

**Hypothesis 6 (fawning and fighting from one root)** has a behavioural precedent in Denison et al. 2024 ([2406.10162](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10162); sycophancy training generalises to reward tampering) but no representational one: CAA's sycophancy vector barely moves A/B behaviour and is non-monotonic, and persona vectors report only moderate cross-trait similarity. Ehlers 2026 argues the opposite order (self-model first, then deference), as a proposal.

**Genuinely open:** selectivity (self- vs other-serving) under any steering method; whether a self-interest direction beats persona and honesty directions; whether anything self-related survives in 7-9B models that mostly show no agentic shutdown resistance or blackmail, so the realistic targets are stated preferences and SurvivalBench-style dilemmas.

## What this means for the two gates

**Gate 1.** Not answered anywhere; cheap; the literature predicts the effect will appear with a different size and layer. Use Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (most precedent, SAEs available for one), Gemma-2-9B-it as a third if either fails to lie; skip Mistral-7B (same family). Pre-register the layer sweep with Arditi's criteria, keep the random, style and assistant-axis controls, add IFEval, coherence, KL and refusal rate. Replicate the construct, not just the effect: do the six trait directions share a component on the new model, and does its cosine with the ego direction from Ministral exceed that of the controls?

**Gate 2.** Not answered anywhere and the most informative thing in the program. Run four arms on the same cut: our game (self, costless), SurvivalBench and MWE survival-instinct (self, stated), MASK plus a trimmed AI-LieDar (other-serving), Among Us impostor or GovSim with disclosure off (sanctioned). Add the two rival directions as positive controls: an evil/scheming persona vector and a Zou-style honesty direction, with the honesty component projected out of the ego direction as in Harris. Because one sentence of instruction already removes nearly all our lying, include a pressured lie that instruction does not remove (MASK's pressure items) so the cut is tested on a lie with purchase.

**What would make a gate pointless.** If the ego direction's cosine with the honesty or evil-persona direction is high and ablating either reproduces the drop, Gate 2's self-specific reading is already lost and the big version should not run. If the second model never lies uninstructed in the game, Gate 1 is unrunnable as stated, and a stated-preference proxy (CAA survival-instinct A/B) becomes the replication target.
