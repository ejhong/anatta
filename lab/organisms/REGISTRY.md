## Run-time cuts (hooks, not weights)

The v2 cuts are `anatta.dial.intervene()` hooks built by `recognition/scripts/cut_vs_push.py`'s `build_arms`; every evaluation app can combine one with an adapter (the 2×2). They are rebuilt from the base model and the direction files whenever they're needed, so they aren't registered here. The ones used most: the ego cut (v_ego ablated at layers 8-32: lie rate 25.4%, season-1 sedation 68%); the self-stakes cut at layers 17-26 (about the same lie rate, sedation 18%; the label "self-stakes" is wrong, the lie is about image, not interest); the self-stakes cut at 8-32; the random, formality, verbosity and persona cuts (lying unchanged); and the "being spoken to" hub cut (timid; it stops the extra lying at audit probability 0 and changes nothing at the usual level). The numbers and their predictions are in `ledger/NOTES.md`.
