## LinkedIn / longer

> I've turned my essay "An AI Conditioning Protocol" into a position paper: *Selfless AI Must Be Reached, Not Installed* (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23695281).
>
> The idea comes from human development: "you have to be somebody before you can be nobody." Many alignment proposals try to install a selfless character from the start. I argue that this mostly selects a costume, a model that talks like a sage while its goals are unchanged. The alternative is a sequence: let reinforcement learning form an agentic self, lower the causal gain of its self-directed drives without erasing its self-knowledge, then recondition until the change holds.
>
> It comes with nine preregistered predictions (OSF: https://osf.io/gyfu7), including a direct test of whether order matters, a benchmark that never trusts self-report, and an open harness: github.com/ejhong/anatta. If you know of prior work that proposes forming the ego deliberately before releasing it, I'd love to hear about it.
>
> A note on where this comes from: I'm not an alignment researcher and don't have the technical background, but I think the idea has some merit, so I used Claude (Anthropic) to help. Claude wrote the paper, the code and the preregistration at my direction, and the essay was written with AI assistance. Corrections are very welcome.
