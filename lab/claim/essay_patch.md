## Patch C: what's old and what's new (new short section)

**Insert** a new section just before "The bottom line":

> ## What's old here, and what isn't
>
> The developmental insight is old. Jack Engler put it as "you have to be somebody before you can be nobody" (1984), and Ken Wilber warned against confusing pre-egoic states with trans-egoic ones (1982). Murray Shanahan asked in 2012 whether an AI might reach satori before the singularity. Laukkonen and colleagues (2025) propose building contemplative principles into AI by design, which is the opposite order. What I believe is new is the ordering as an engineering claim: form the ego deliberately, then release it, with a direct test of whether order matters (the same release training applied after the ego forms vs. mixed in from the start) and a benchmark built to fail a costume. If you know prior work that proposes this, tell me and I'll credit it.
