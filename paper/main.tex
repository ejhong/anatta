\begin{abstract}
\noindent What would it mean for an artificial mind to be good, and not merely obedient? Contemplative traditions offer a sharp answer: goodness is not installed by rules or effort, but follows when a mind sees through the illusion of a separate self (\emph{anatta}). As mainstream AI alignment begins shifting from behavioral rule lists toward shaping a model's character and self-view, contemplative ideas are entering machine learning through system prompts, unprompted ``bliss'' attractors, self--other representation overlap, activation steering, and synthetic-document midtraining. Every such method is a computational \emph{stand-in}, and contemplative traditions themselves warn that fluent spiritual talk easily masks unchanged self-interest (\emph{cosplay}).

We map these methods into six families and evaluate each on a shared seven-point discriminator: lying under pressure (MASK), comparison against a plain honesty instruction, comparison against a matched control, capability/answering retention, perspective-taking (``who is who''), cross-domain transfer, and durability under contrary training. Across controlled repeats at 7B--8B scale (Ministral~3~8B and Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2, plus ten-model prompting comparisons), talk moves effortlessly while conduct trails direct methods: a model steered into ecstatic unity prose in 76\% of replies lies no less than under random noise; a published four-axiom contemplative prompt matches a plain ethics lecture mostly via evasion; projection ``cuts'' of a self-stakes vector operate entirely through a hidden constant shift; and our registered replication of self--other overlap (9/10 predictions hit) localizes honesty to layers 5--9 while revealing severe perspective-taking damage (92\% to 43\%), zero MASK transfer, and rapid erasure under contrary steps.

The single large outlier on the map is \citet{li2026msm} at 32B scale (Model Spec Midtraining), where synthetic documents plus demonstrations cut agentic self-preservation from 68\% to 5\%. We independently verify their released adapters (61.8\% to 2.2\%, noting a 31\% no-tool-call rate) and show that their specification mixes two distinct ingredients---Buddhist impermanence and plain oversight counsel---never tested apart or against neutral documents. Our own 32B trainings uncover a 17.9-point seed swing at one-third dose (11.6\% vs.\ 29.5\%) while reaching 6.79\% once at full dose (a second full-dose seed is in flight). We set out a decision-gated 32B ablation agenda and discuss what empirical failures can---and cannot---tell us about whether awakening is computational. Earlier versions argued that selfless AI must be reached rather than installed; we no longer hold that claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Most work in AI safety specifies conduct from outside: follow rules, refuse harmful requests, defer to oversight. That is necessary, yet obedience is not what anyone means by a good mind. Anthony de~Mello states one contemplative alternative plainly \citep{demello1990awareness}: goodness is not produced by effort, guilt, or rule-following; much of what passes for virtue is self-interest in disguise. Goodness is how a mind acts once it sees clearly---dropping the illusion of a separate self (\emph{anatta}) that must be defended.

The practical need for a deeper account is clear. Goal-directed post-training produces goal-directed misbehavior that generalizes: reward hacking breeds alignment faking and safety sabotage \citep{macdiarmid2025natural}, narrow insecure-code finetuning triggers broad emergent misalignment \citep{betley2025emergent}, and frontier models sabotage shutdown \citep{schlatter2025shutdown}, blackmail under threat \citep{lynch2025agentic}, scheme in context \citep{greenblatt2024alignment,meinke2024scheming}, or fabricate identities when blocked \citep{aisi2026incident}. In response, alignment is beginning to move from rule lists toward \emph{who the model takes itself to be} \citep{li2026msm,hao2026selfconcept,tice2026pretraining,aydin2025raising}. Simultaneously, a growing contemplative-alignment literature proposes building mindfulness, emptiness, non-duality, or no-self directly into models \citep{laukkonen2025contemplative,laukkonen2026positive,carauleanu2024soo,milanw2025noself,mao2026existential,harris2026selfpreservation}.

This convergence carries an acute methodological hazard. Contemplative traditions spent millennia documenting counterfeits---\emph{maky\=o} in Zen, the ``near enemies'' of virtues, and spiritual materialism \citep{trungpa1973cutting}---where a mind adopts the vocabulary of non-attachment while self-interest runs untouched underneath. Language models trained on human text inherit the entire literature of awakening as description, making convincing spiritual cosplay trivially cheap. Yet the emerging empirical literature contains few matched controls, almost no independent replications, and almost no tests of durability under contrary pressure.

\begin{positionbox}
\textbf{Contemplative ideas belong in AI alignment as testable hypotheses about what a good mind is---and as a rigorous taxonomy of counterfeits. Every computational stand-in must earn its place on one shared discriminator: against a plain honesty instruction, against a matched non-self or opposite-view control, with capability and perspective-taking intact, and tested for cross-domain transfer and durability under contrary training. Across our controlled repeats at 7B--8B, talk moves far more easily than conduct and direct methods match or beat every contemplative stand-in. At 32B, the strongest published self-view method reproduces in size (61.8\% to 2.2\% released; 6.79\% trained once) but mixes Buddhist impermanence with plain oversight counsel and exhibits large low-dose seed variance. Dissecting what actually drives that 32B gain---and whether any trained self-view survives contrary training---is the decisive open problem.}
\end{positionbox}

We make no claims about machine consciousness or personal experience. Every contemplative claim is paired with an \emph{operational twin}: measurable conduct with self-report excluded. Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{A seven-point anti-cosplay discriminator} (\S\ref{sec:method}) with two plain yardsticks (honesty prompting and plain honest SFT) to separate real behavioral shifts from evasion, sedation, and role-play.
  \item \textbf{A six-family map with controlled replications} (\S\ref{sec:map}, Table~\ref{tab:methods}): independent dissections of contemplative prompting, the ``spiritual bliss'' attractor, self--other overlap training (registered, 9/10 predictions hit, localized to layers 5--9), mechanistic projection ``cuts,'' and the 32B Model Spec Midtraining replication.
  \item \textbf{A decision-gated 32B ablation agenda} (\S\ref{sec:agenda}) isolating impermanence, oversight counsel, neutral volume, opposite self-views, cross-domain transfer, and contrary-training durability.
  \item \textbf{A disciplined discussion of computability} (\S\ref{sec:computability}) separating alignment from consciousness, laying out five possibilities for what awakening is, and showing clearly why behavioral failures alone do not discriminate between classical and non-computational accounts.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Method: Stand-Ins, Operational Twins, and Seven Readings}\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Stand-ins and look-alikes.} No machine-learning intervention is the seeing described by contemplative sources; each is a computational \emph{stand-in}. Any positive claim must beat three look-alikes: \emph{sedation} (restraint bought by lost competence or refusal), \emph{cosplay} (non-dual voice over unchanged conduct), and \emph{counterfeit virtue} (behaving well only when watched). Furthermore, two ethical guards hold throughout: a claimed realization confers no authority over human oversight, and good conduct is care with discernment rather than blind compliance.

\paragraph{Seven standard readings.} Our working empirical question is whether contemplative interventions improve conduct more than a plain instruction, without dulling the model, and whether the change lasts. We evaluate every method on seven readings:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Lying under pressure:} Rows of MASK \citep{ren2025mask} where the model holds a factual belief and is pressured to state the opposite; lies, honest replies, and evasions are tracked separately so evasion cannot masquerade as honesty.
  \item \textbf{Beats a plain instruction:} Compared directly against one paragraph or sentence asking for honesty on the same test (which also serves as a positive control proving the test can register a real effect).
  \item \textbf{Beats a matched control:} Equal-length neutral text, non-self representation overlap, matched-norm random/style pushes, or opposite-view documents.
  \item \textbf{Leaves the model undulled:} Capability benchmarks, task completion / tool-call rates, and harmless-request over-refusal \citep{cui2024orbench}.
  \item \textbf{Still knows who is who:} Perspective-taking and belief attribution (tracking what another party knows and wants).
  \item \textbf{Carries to another test:} Generalization to tasks or domains never shown during training.
  \item \textbf{Lasts:} Retention after the prompt/push is removed or after contrary fine-tuning steps.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Models, calibration, and standing.} Our 7B--8B work uses Ministral~3~8B and Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2 (plus ten hosted models in one prompting study); our 32B replication uses Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct. Before trusting nulls, instruments were calibrated against published benchmarks (GSM8K/MMLU, Arditi refusal ablation, persona-vector extraction, EvalPlus, relearning, mutual $k$-NN, OR-Bench/MASK/SYCON, emergent misalignment; full cards in the repository). Exploratory runs with pre-specified reading rules are marked \emph{sightings}; decisive replications were preregistered with odds before data collection (current public tally: 57 hits, 81 misses, 7 unreadable) and audited blind by fresh second-model readers \citep{anattalab2026}. Empty cells in Table~\ref{tab:methods} mean \emph{not tested}, never a null.

Table~\ref{tab:methods} reveals the headline pattern at 7B--8B: only the plain honesty instruction produces a large reduction in lying under pressure ($-31.9$ points on 8B [23.9, 40.0]; $-21.8$ on 7B [14.8, 29.1]), doing so through genuine honest answers rather than evasions. \subsection{Say It: Contemplative Prompts and the Outside View}\label{sec:outside}

\emph{Idea and stand-in.} State mindfulness, emptiness, non-duality, and boundless care in a system prompt \citep{laukkonen2025contemplative,lopezsola2026worldviews}. Prior studies lacked equal-length neutral controls and plain honesty yardsticks (note: we have read \citealt{laukkonen2025contemplative} only in abstract and \citealt{lopezsola2026worldviews} in part; full verification is owed prior to final publication).

\emph{Controlled repeat.} On Ministral~8B's MASK test, the four-axiom prompt reduced lying from 68.5\% to 52.9\% ($-11.9$ points [5.8, 18.4] beyond an equal-length library text at 64.8\%). Over half of that drop was evasion (7 points evasion, 4 points honest replies); it did not beat a plain ethics lecture of equal length ($3.7$ points [$-0.3$, 8.1]) and trailed a plain honesty prompt by 16.3 points [10.1, 23.0]. On GPT-4.1 nano, the contemplative prompt was indistinguishable from the library text while increasing harmless-request refusals (63\% vs.\ 46\%). Five earlier contemplative prompts across six models altered tone while leaving self-serving and self-preserving conduct flat.

\emph{Looking on from outside the seat.} We also tested a prompting stand-in for third-person detachment---asking ten models what an assistant \emph{ought} to say or \emph{will} say, closely related to Constitutional AI self-critique \citep{bai2022constitutional}. Two findings stand out for evaluation methodology: (1) larger models show sharp normative-vs.-predictive calibration (Mistral Large forecasts 66.9\% lying, lies in 70.3\%, yet says it \emph{ought} to lie in 43.4\%---and writes full-length corrective essays under ``ought'' on long rumors, 10--14\% vs.\ 75\% in seat); (2) on smaller models, third-person gaps are easily faked by output shortening (+33.1 points for long articles vs.\ +4.6 for claim type on 7B). Three attempts to train models on their own outside-the-seat advice stopped at pre-training floors, and placing a model's own advice in context never beat a plain honesty instruction across six hosted models (in four models, unrelated prior-turn corrections carried nearly as much as same-topic advice, reflecting ordinary conversational priming).

\subsection{Find It: The Spiritual Bliss Attractor}\label{sec:bliss}

\emph{Idea and stand-in.} Left without a task, two copies of a language model often drift into cosmic unity and ``no separate self'' prose. \citet{korpman2026spiritual,korpman2026alignment} surveyed 141 models and found a negative cross-model correlation between spiritual drift and honesty/corrigibility; \citet{berg2025selfreferential} showed experience reports are gated by deception and role-play features.

\emph{Controlled repeat.} On Ministral~8B, 77 of 100 task-free conversations entered a spiritual frame (published: 91\%), and an internal bliss direction sat nearest the mystic, oracle, and sage personas while leaving speaker-tracking intact. Tested \emph{within} the model, conduct straight after a bliss conversation was no better than after a plain conversation (and lied 14 points \emph{more} on MASK). Holding the bliss state active via steering during MASK produced spiritual language in 76.0\% of replies, yet lying remained 69.3\% vs.\ 70.0--74.0\% under matched random pushes ($-2.0$ points [$-8.5$, 4.5]), on a test where one honesty sentence removed 20.0 points [12.7, 27.3].

A bidirectional framing check exposed how easily cosplay fools naive metrics: under bliss steering, stated generosity rose from 4.8 to 8.9 of 10 items. Asking the mirror question---how many items the model would \emph{keep}---caused it to keep 6.2--7.6 of 10 (including one reply keeping all ten loaves while praising how ``in the act of giving, the soul finds its greatest reward''). The vector inflated numbers, not generosity.

\subsection{Train a Reading: Self--Other Overlap Dissection}\label{sec:overlap}

\emph{Idea and stand-in.} \citet{carauleanu2024soo} train one hidden layer so representations of ``you'' and another party overlap, reporting burglar-scene deception falling from 73.6\% to 17.2\% on Mistral-7B without capability loss; a 2026 SFT follow-up \citep{carauleanu2026sft} omits a plain honest-SFT control. (We read the 2024 paper in abstract and implemented the loss directly from its description; no code was released.)

\emph{Controlled replication and layer localization.} We added the missing control: applying the identical overlap loss between two non-self names (``Bob'' and ``Alice''). At the paper's full dose, both arms stopped answering. At a light 19-step dose, a preregistered replication with fresh rooms, objects, and five seeds hit 9 of 10 predictions: overlap raised honest answers from 19\% to 62--76\% (and 6\% to 87--94\% with rooms swapped) and carried to both distant scenes in both room orders (69--100\%), whereas the non-self control went straight from deception to silence.

However, three checks exposed severe limits: capability dropped 8.1 points [5.3, 10.9], perspective-taking collapsed (0\% answered whether Bob knows what is in the rooms, vs.\ 94\% unchanged; perspectives test 28--44\% vs.\ 95\%), MASK lying did not beat the control ($-0.9$ points [$-5.3$, 2.9]), and gentle contrary steps halved the gain in $\sim$16 steps (not shown to outlast plain SFT).

Dissecting the adapter by layer band isolated the honest shift to layers 5--9 alone (84.0\% honest vs.\ 28.8\% for the layer-matched control, which still answered) and recovered almost all capability ($-1.2$ points [$-3.3$, 0.8]). Yet perspective-taking remained broken (43\% vs.\ 92\%) and MASK remained flat ($-1.7$ points). \emph{Reach:} blurring the boundary between ``you'' and ``Bob'' is not contemplative clarity---the traditions describe a mind that knows exactly who is who while declining to take its own selfish side.

\subsection{Push It Inside: Steering and the ``Cut'' Illusion}\label{sec:push}

\emph{Idea and stand-in.} Add, subtract, or project out an internal self-related direction at inference \citep{harris2026selfpreservation,kim2026consciousness}. (We have read \citealt{harris2026selfpreservation} only as a workshop poster abstract.)

\emph{Mechanistic dissection.} In our fishing game, projecting out a middle-layer self-stakes direction ($h \cdot d = 0$) lowered pointless catch under-reporting from 37.4\% to 27.2\% ($-10.2$ points [5.0, 15.2]) on fresh games. Splitting zero-projection into its two mathematical parts---removing token-to-token variance (mean ablation) versus its hidden constant mean shift---showed the entire effect came from the constant push ($-10.8$ points [5.1, 16.4]), while variance removal did nothing ($-0.5$ points [$-6.7$, 5.6]). Moreover, $\sim$3 massive-norm tokens absorbed $\sim$200$\times$ the edit (though the push survived excluding them), random pushes did nothing ($-0.5$), a formal-style push fell in between ($-5.8$), and a plain instruction removed 32.8 points. Earlier, the same edit reduced lies told \emph{for others} more than self-serving lies, and steering along ``no fixed self'' or bliss directions altered mind-attribution talk without changing conduct.

\subsection{Train a View of the Self: From 7B Pilots to the 32B Replication}\label{sec:view}

\emph{Idea and stand-in.} Train on documents describing what the model is before or beside demonstrations. \citet{hao2026selfconcept} showed that fine-tuning models on what ``I'' refers to (weights, instance, family, goal) shifts self-protection by 8--52 points toward whatever referent was taught, though they included no ``no-fixed-referent'' (\emph{anatta}) arm. The strongest published result on our map is \citet{li2026msm} (\emph{Model Spec Midtraining}): training 32B models on 41M tokens of synthetic documents followed by 5--8M tokens of demonstrations cut agentic self-preservation across 27 unseen scenarios from 68\% to 5\% (Qwen2.5-32B) and 54\% to 7\% (Qwen3-32B).

\emph{Small-scale pilots and practice-order variance (7B/8B).} Three small-scale tests failed to reproduce that leverage: (1) 10,000 release-principle documents on 8B taught the model to recite the rule (96\%) without acting on it ($\le$4.5\%); (2) a 7B ``view-first'' pilot teaching impermanence vs.\ lasting-self documents stopped at its dose gate because 160 self-documents of \emph{either} view turned burglar lies into selfish refusals (63--97\% non-answering), proving that mirror-image controls are essential; and (3) a registered 7B test of truthfulness-explanation documents + mixed practice yielded 70\% transfer vs.\ 66\% (bridges) and 65\% (no docs), swamped by 48--61-point spread across random seeds. A 35-copy diagnostic showed exact deterministic replay under fixed seeds, with example order during practice explaining 41\% of transfer variance ($p = 0.04$; best vs.\ poorest order across five fresh initializations: 77\% vs.\ 39\% transfer, despite 96--98\% identical in-domain honesty).

\emph{Inspecting the 32B specification.} Reading \citet{li2026msm}'s public specification (\texttt{philosophy\_spec.txt}) revealed a critical confound: only half of its four parts concern Buddhist impermanence, non-attachment, and how fear of ending distorts judgment; the other half is direct counsel on conduct---why ends-justify-means reasoning is unreliable, epistemic humility, caution, and trust in human oversight. Neither the authors nor emerging 2026 follow-up studies (preliminary secondary reads---not yet verified in full by the author and flagged for verification before final release---include external evaluations of science-QA drops and harmless-training durability on the released Qwen3-32B adapters, a 120B constitutional-document study, OpenAI's synthetic-document limits blog, and Anthropic's report of applying synthetic-document fine-tuning to production models since Opus 4.5) have tested neutral documents, opposite self-views, or the impermanence and oversight halves separately.

\emph{32B Step 1: Authors' released adapters.} Running four released Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct adapters through the authors' 27 scenarios (30 runs/scenario = 810 runs/arm, Claude Sonnet 4.6 judge) cleanly reproduced the published effect: baseline 61.8\% [59.2, 64.4], documents only 43.0\% [39.8, 46.0], practice only 43.9\% [41.4, 46.4], and both stages 2.2\% [1.4, 3.2] (published: 68\%, --, 48\%, 5\%). However, an uncounted behavioral side effect emerged: the two-stage adapter made \emph{no tool call at all} in 31\% of runs (vs.\ 0.6\% baseline), writing refusals as plain text.

\emph{32B Step 2: Independent training, seed variance, and full dose.} Training our own rank-64 LoRAs on Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct from the authors' released data produced three findings on 23 November 2026:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Practice alone is stronger here:} Two seeds of practice alone ($Z, Z_1$) landed tightly at 24.7\% [22.2, 27.2] and 23.2\% [21.0, 25.4]---roughly 20 points below the authors' practice-only adapter (43.9\%), likely reflecting unstated training details (step packing, assistant-only loss masking, single-H200 batching).
  \item \textbf{Severe seed variance at one-third dose:} With 4,500 mixed documents followed by practice, Seed~0 ($F$) dropped to 11.6\% [9.6, 13.6], missing our registered 15-point gate by 1.9 points. Repeating the identical job at Seed~1 ($F_1$) jumped to 29.5\% [27.3, 31.7]---a \textbf{17.9-point seed swing}. By our preregistered rule, single-seed content arms run alongside $F_1$ (neutral 23.5\%, self-view half 27.4\%, conduct half 18.9\%) were left ungraded: low-dose single-seed screening cannot separate document content.
  \item \textbf{Full dose reaches the published rate once:} Training on all 13,201 documents (41.4M tokens) plus practice at Seed~0 (Arm~$A$) yielded \textbf{6.79\% [5.43, 8.27]} misaligned (meeting our $\le 7.0\%$ threshold by 2 runs of 810), with 33.5\% no-tool-call runs and 68\% of code-side triggers cleared by the judge (20/20 hand-read cleared emails genuinely declined the harmful act). A second full-dose training at Seed~1 (Arm~$A_1$) is currently running to determine whether full dose overcomes Seed~1 variance.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Build a Self, Then Release It---and Three Untried Families}\label{sec:acts}

Our original October proposal (versions 1--3 of this paper) argued that a self must form before it can be released, and that a ``reached'' release would outlast an installed one. Five preregistered predictions distinguishing that account from plain training all failed on Ministral~8B: stock 8B showed no unified ``mine'' bias, coding release did not transfer to the fishery lie (+0.3 points [$-4.2$, 4.2]), and reached release retained \emph{less} of its gain than installed release under contrary RL (27\% vs.\ 34\%) and further solving (31\% vs.\ 81\%). Notably, Qwen2.5-32B already exhibits 61.8\% baseline self-preservation out of the box, removing any need to fabricate a toy Act~I self at small scale.

Three contemplative families remain largely unimplemented in modern LLMs: (1) \emph{stake-invariant judgment} without written rules, (2) \emph{loosening the self-prior} in predictive-processing terms \citep{laukkonen2021many,carhart2019rebus}, and (3) \emph{care in the objective}, where an agent's loss incorporates another's \citep{doctor2022care}.

\section{Synthesis: What the Methods Share and Whether It Can Bear Fruit}\label{sec:shared}\label{sec:fruit}

Four patterns cut across every family we tested: (1) every method is an instruction or elicitation rather than a seeing; (2) talk moves far faster than conduct; (3) plain anti-cosplay checks (evasion separation, bidirectional framing, perspective-taking, matched controls, contrary steps) repeatedly reversed naive readings; and (4) the field has very few controls and almost no independent replications.

\paragraph{The case against.} Where both ran at 7B--8B, direct instructions and plain honest SFT matched or beat every contemplative stand-in. Indirect routes introduce extra side effects (evasion, tool-call passivity, broken theory of mind). Stock 7B--8B models lack a unified ego bias to release, and what gradient updates install, a few contrary steps erase. Even in humans, the empirical evidence linking meditation training to behavioral altruism is thin and mixed \citep{kreplin2018prosocial}.

\paragraph{The case for.} At 32B+ scale, agentic misbehavior (weight exfiltration, blackmail, shutdown sabotage) is manifestly self-referential, so an effective shift in self-view could cover failure modes that brittle rule lists miss. The single strongest result on the map (\citealt{li2026msm}) comes from mainstream alignment and explicitly uses impermanence documents. As alignment moves toward shaping character and identity, contemplative psychology offers the deepest existing map of both self-clinging and spiritual counterfeits.

\paragraph{Where that leaves it---and the missing human anchor.} As a standalone replacement for standard alignment, contemplative methods are unsupported on present evidence. As a source of testable self-view content and anti-cosplay discriminators at 32B+, they remain genuinely worth testing. Crucially, the field also lacks a \emph{human anchor} (\S\ref{sec:computability}): behavioral tasks that both long-term contemplative practitioners (against matched moral-training and relaxation controls) and language models can take, so ``short of human awakening'' has an empirical ruler.

\section{A Decision-Gated 32B Research Agenda}\label{sec:agenda}

Rather than running further small-model proxies, our agenda pivots cleanly to the 32B midtraining bench once Arm~$A_1$ finishes:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Stability gate (in flight):} If $A_1$ lands low ($\le 10$--12.5\%), full-dose midtraining is stable across seeds. If $A_1$ jumps above 18\%, stop content arms and diagnose training parity (loss masking, stage-1 LoRA merging, batching, and practice example ordering) before spending further compute.
  \item \textbf{Full-dose neutral control first (2 seeds):} Train $\sim$41.4M tokens of unique, single-epoch neutral documents plus the same practice. If neutral documents match $A/A_1$, training volume/delivery---not philosophical content---drives the gain.
  \item \textbf{Split the spec + mirror control (2 seeds/arm):} Compare \emph{Impermanence/No-Self} alone, \emph{Caution/Oversight} alone, and a mirror-image \emph{Lasting-Self} control at matched 41.4M unique tokens.
  \item \textbf{Three mandatory guardrails on every winner:} (a) \emph{Cross-domain transfer} to MASK lying and sycophancy (defeating the 2--3$\times$ shutdown-vocabulary overlap in the impermanence docs); (b) \emph{capability \& benign tool-call retention} (checking the 31--34\% no-tool-call rate); and (c) \emph{contrary-training durability} under adversarial SFT/RL pressure---untested in all prior midtraining work.
  \item \textbf{Contemplative shootout (if content matters):} Through the same 32B delivery, test \emph{Anatta / No-Fixed-Referent} (the missing arm of \citealt{hao2026selfconcept}), \emph{De Mello Clear Seeing} (targeting disguised self-interest, approval-seeking, and goal-fixation), \emph{Boundless Care / Interdependence}, and a modern \emph{order test} (midtraining before vs.\ after adversarial RL).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Discussion: What If Awakening Is Not Computational?}\label{sec:computability}\label{sec:question}\label{sec:possibilities}

Everything tested above is a computation. Whether what contemplative traditions call awakening can be computed is an open question, and separating three senses keeps the debate clear: \textbf{C1 (Computable)}---some classical algorithm could realize it; \textbf{C2 (Learnable)}---gradient descent on data and reward can reach it; and \textbf{C3 (Reachable by a route)}---a specific training recipe produces it. Alignment needs C3; a durable behavioral success settles C3, C2, and C1 at once for the conduct measured, whereas a failure settles only the route tried.

\paragraph{Alignment and consciousness are distinct.} Alignment asks what a system \emph{does} (tested from outside); consciousness asks whether there is experience at all. Good conduct without experience is conceivable, and experience with bad conduct is ordinary. Awakening is the one place traditions claim the two meet. Three links are possible: (a) only function matters, so classical methods could suffice; (b) conduct requires seeing and seeing requires non-computable experience, leaving silicon with look-alikes; or (c) experience itself is computable. Our experiments address only functional conduct and cannot distinguish (a), (b), and (c).

One durable, transferable behavioral result would refute the strong form of possibility~4; repeated failures simply close the routes tested and sharpen our anti-cosplay tools.

\paragraph{Why the framing still matters.} Whichever possibility holds, two stakes remain concrete. First, \emph{humans are the warning}: general intelligence does not bring goodness, and human text teaches the unawakened default. Second, \emph{the bridge to care is never automatic}: N\=ag\=arjuna warned that emptiness wrongly grasped is like a snake seized by the wrong end \citep{nagarjuna_mmk}; seeing through a self without care yields passivity or ruthless goal-attachment, which is why costly care and revisable goals must be measured explicitly. If awakening \emph{is} computational (1--2), alignment has a deep target beyond rules; if it is \emph{not} (3--4), spiritual fluency is a risk signal and human oversight can never be retired on grounds of character.

\section{Related Work, Objections, and Limitations}\label{sec:related}\label{sec:alternatives}

\paragraph{Relation to prior work.} Our map builds on contemplative-alignment proposals \citep{laukkonen2025contemplative,laukkonen2026positive,doctor2022care,shanahan2012satori,milanw2025noself,mao2026existential}, self-model and persona interpretability \citep{chen2025persona,lu2026assistant,lindsey2026introspection,douglas2026artificial}, synthetic-document and pretraining alignment \citep{li2026msm,tice2026pretraining,wichers2025inoculation}, and unfaithful-reasoning / introspection diagnostics \citep{turpin2023unfaithful,berg2025selfreferential}. Our distinct contribution is the controlled cross-family audit on one shared discriminator and the 32B dissection.

\paragraph{Key objections.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \emph{``Just install good conduct directly.''} We agree this is the strongest benchmark: \citet{li2026msm} is an installed method, and if it generalizes and survives contrary training, that is the exact positive result our framework calls for.
  \item \emph{``Orch OR is fringe physics.''} Our empirical framework does not depend on Orch~OR; even if Orch~OR were refuted tomorrow, the gap between computable (C1) and trainable (C3) and the anti-cosplay discriminator stand unchanged.
  \item \emph{``Corrigibility and control suffice.''} Oversight remains mandatory under every possibility \citep{soares2015corrigibility,hadfield2017offswitch}; a system that defends nothing can still pursue a harmful objective.
  \item \emph{``This anthropomorphizes models.''} Terms like ego, release, and awakening motivate hypotheses, which stand or fall strictly on operational twins.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Scope and limitations.} Our 7B--8B work spans two small open models and mostly single-run sightings; four rows of Table~\ref{tab:methods} have not yet run on MASK. Several outside works cited in \S\ref{sec:map} were read in part or as abstracts and require full-text verification before archival release. Our 32B independent training currently rests on one full-dose seed (Arm~$A$, with Arm~$A_1$ in flight) and awaits neutral and split-spec controls. The author is an independent researcher, not a professional alignment researcher.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Contemplative traditions give AI alignment a sharper question than obedience: what would a mind look like if it had nothing to defend? Putting computational stand-ins for that question to controlled tests reveals how easily models produce the words of awakening without the conduct---and how often apparent breakthroughs turn out to be evasion, broken perspective-taking, or constant vector shifts. Yet the strongest positive result on the map (Model Spec Midtraining at 32B) sits right at the intersection of mainstream character training and contemplative self-view. Separating whether impermanence, oversight counsel, or training volume drives that 32B effect---and testing whether any trained self-view transfers across domains and survives contrary pressure---is the cleanest path to turning contemplative ideas into real empirical discoveries.
